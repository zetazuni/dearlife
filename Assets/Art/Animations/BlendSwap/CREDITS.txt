Walks and the idle of the people, baked onto one standard skeleton by tools/blender_anim.py (skeleton and motion only,
none of the original models or textures are in this project).

- walk_bs_man.fbx: the 40 frame walk cycle of "Rigged Man With Walk Cycle" by brightonpiers,
  https://blendswap.com/blend/3764, Creative Commons Attribution (a modification of the Low Poly Character by Blork).
- walkf_bs_sintel.fbx: the walk cycle "Walk_A" of "Sintel Walkcycle" by Shader, https://blendswap.com/blend/3090, CC0,
  on the Sintel rig of the Blender Foundation's Durian open movie project.
- stand_made_idle.fbx: made for this game by tools/blender_anim.py (a relaxed stand with breathing and a slight shift
  of weight), no recording.
